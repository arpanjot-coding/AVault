A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
make the call.

Need a hand? ↗
Is AVault free and open source?+

Yes. AVault is free to use, with source code available under the GNU General Public License v3 or later. You can inspect the implementation and contribute on GitHub.

Do I need to root my phone?+

No. AVault uses Shizuku to access the system services needed for recording. You will need to start Shizuku and grant the required permissions during setup.

Will it record every call on every phone?+

Recording support varies across manufacturers, Android versions and calling apps. Third-party call recording can be limited by the calling app itself. Make a test recording to confirm your configuration.

What happens when I share a recording?+

Sharing an encrypted recording creates a decrypted audio copy for the app you select. That copy is outside the protection of your vault. Share only with a destination you trust.

Can I choose how long recordings are kept?+

Yes. Enable auto-delete and choose a retention period in settings. Bookmarked recordings are excluded from automatic deletion. You can also leave auto-delete off.
