## Technical limitations and user responsibility

Android, device manufacturers and calling apps can change recording behaviour. Recordings can be missing, incomplete, silent, one-sided, inaccurate or stored unexpectedly. Concurrent and held calls, unavailable caller identities and operating-system changes can affect detection and recording filters. Monitor your own deployment and stop any activity that you are not authorised to perform.

Encryption, app lock and screen privacy do not guarantee protection against every threat. Plaintext can exist during recording finalisation and in temporary playback/share files. A receiving app controls its own shared copies. Deleting a file does not guarantee forensic erasure. Clearing app data, uninstalling or losing Keystore access can prevent recovery.

Test only with appropriate permission before relying on a configuration. Do not rely on AVault as the sole record for a safety-critical, legal, medical or other consequential decision. Maintain appropriate independent records and obtain qualified advice where needed. Nothing in this documentation is legal, security or professional advice for a particular deployment.
